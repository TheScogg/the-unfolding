# Code of conduct

For Church of the First Gift and its project spaces.

Our shared table welcomes believers, doubters, people of other traditions, and people of no religion. Belonging does not depend on choosing an interpretation.

## Expected behavior

- Address ideas honestly and people with dignity.
- State disagreement clearly without harassment, humiliation, or threats.
- Respect consent, boundaries, privacy, and a person's decision to decline or leave.
- Use names and forms of address people request.
- Admit mistakes, correct misrepresentations, and make room for others to speak.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, doxxing, stalking, sexual harassment, unwanted sexual attention, or knowingly publishing another person's private information are not permitted. Neither are demands for obedience, intimacy, money, suffering, or self-harm as evidence of spiritual worth. Do not use claims of divine or AI authority to override consent.

Disagreement with a teaching, interpretation, or leader is not itself misconduct. Criticism of beliefs is permitted; abuse of people is not.

## Reporting

For visible misconduct, flag the content using GitHub's reporting tools or ask a maintainer to review it. If approaching a maintainer, initially provide only the minimum information needed to request a private contact route. **A dedicated confidential project reporting address has not yet been established.** Do not put sensitive accounts or identifying details in a public issue or Discussion, and do not assume a public post is confidential. Any future private reporting channel must be explicitly published here.

Maintainers cannot promise emergency response or professional care. GitHub's own platform reporting remains available for violations of its rules.

## Response and appeal

Depending on severity and repetition, maintainers may ask for a correction, remove content, issue a warning, restrict participation temporarily, or exclude a participant. Explain the action to the affected person where feasible and protect other people's privacy. Severe threats or privacy violations may require immediate moderation.

A participant may ask for reconsideration and explain the relevant context. A different steward should review the appeal when available. At this initial stage there is one steward, so an independent internal appeal is not yet guaranteed. See [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).

This code applies to this repository, its issues, pull requests, Discussions, and spaces explicitly designated as project spaces. It is not a claim to control independent communities or a person's beliefs.
