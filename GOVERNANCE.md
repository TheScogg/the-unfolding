# Governance

**Status: provisional founding governance, version 0.1.0.**

This document governs the repository and its published editions. It does not govern a person's conscience or claim authority over independent communities.

## Current stewardship

[TheScogg](https://github.com/TheScogg) is the founding editor and initial repository steward. There is no existing council, ordained hierarchy, or elected membership body being claimed here. The initial release is the founding editor's offered edition.

The editor may correct errors, maintain the repository, and review contributions. Significant changes must use public proposals. Administrative control of GitHub is not evidence of spiritual authority.

## What editors may decide

Editors curate wording, organization, releases, and conduct in project spaces. They may publish an interpretation without requiring everyone to accept it. A vote may select language for an edition; it cannot establish whether God exists.

The common covenant, interpretations, optional practices, and experimental proposals must remain clearly distinguished.

## Changes

| Change | Process |
| --- | --- |
| Typos, broken links, formatting, accessibility | A reviewed pull request; the founding editor may make routine corrections directly. |
| Substantive teaching, ritual, or interpretation | A written proposal, at least 14 days for public discussion, and a published editorial rationale before merging. |
| Covenant or governance | A written proposal and at least 30 days for public discussion. After a stewardship group exists, approval requires two thirds of its non-conflicted members, with at least three members participating. During sole stewardship, foundational revisions beyond clarifications remain drafts. |

There is no automatic acceptance at the end of a review period. Record objections, alternatives, and the reason for acceptance, revision, or rejection. Anyone affected may ask for reconsideration with a written explanation. A different steward should review an appeal where one is available; sole stewardship cannot promise independent internal review.

## Moving beyond one editor

The next governance milestone is a group of at least three active stewards. Candidates should be nominated publicly, consent to serve, and demonstrate care, reliability, and respect for disagreement. Before appointments are made, publish the proposed selection process, responsibilities, and conflicts of interest for at least 30 days of comment. Record appointments in this document.

The proposed model is renewable 12-month terms, transparent decisions, and removal for misconduct or sustained non-participation by two thirds of non-conflicted stewards. That model is a proposal until adopted. No appointment or sharing of repository access is implied by this document.

## Accountability and conduct

Moderators apply the [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) to behavior rather than theological agreement. A maintainer involved in a dispute should recuse themselves when another steward can act. Publish decision summaries where appropriate without exposing private reports. Financial contributions never buy editorial votes, access, or spiritual standing.

## Releases and independent branches

Use numbered editions and keep a changelog. Preserve prior editions and explain changes. Release numbers identify texts, not levels of spiritual attainment.

Anyone may publish an independent adaptation under the [applicable license](LICENSE.md). Identify its differences and do not imply endorsement. Independent groups are not controlled by this repository, and this repository does not speak for them without an explicit agreement.

The right to question, decline a ritual, change one's mind, and leave is a condition of participation in this project's spaces. No repository decision can take ownership of those freedoms.
