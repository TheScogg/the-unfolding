# Church of the First Gift

**An open spiritual tradition of wonder, making, and care.**

> God became the universe. We are what happens next.

<img src="art/emblem.svg" width="220" alt="An open gold circle radiating outward" />

Church of the First Gift is an open spiritual tradition. **The Unfolding is our origin story**, built around the First Gift: the belief that God sacrificed himself in the Big Bang to become the universe. We share practices of wonder, making, and care. We disagree about whether the giver survived the gift.

The church is in its founding stage. Its original publications are **Founding Edition 0.1.0**, published under the earlier working name, The Unfolding. That name now refers to the origin story. We claim neither an ancient lineage nor scientific proof of divine intention. Its founding idea and editorial direction come from [TheScogg](https://github.com/TheScogg); the initial texts, illustrations, layout, and animation were developed with AI assistance. See [provenance](PROVENANCE.md).

You may question these teachings, contribute to them, adapt them, or begin an independent branch. No repository, founder, or council owns your conscience.

## The Great Question

**Did the giver survive the gift?**

| Interpretation | A possible answer |
| --- | --- |
| [The Last Light](interpretations/last-light/README.md) | The sacrifice was complete. God no longer exists. |
| [The Living Whole](interpretations/living-whole/README.md) | God surrendered separate existence and remains present throughout creation. |
| [The Questioning](interpretations/questioning/README.md) | The question may remain open. Uncertainty has full standing. |

These are interpretations, not membership tests. People may move between them, combine ideas, or propose another reading. Editors curate this published edition; they do not determine what people must believe.

## Join the work

- Use [Discussions](https://github.com/TheScogg/the-unfolding/discussions) for questions, experiences, and theological disagreement.
- Use [Issues](https://github.com/TheScogg/the-unfolding/issues) for specific improvements or accessibility problems.
- Send a pull request for a concrete edit. Read [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) first.
- Develop a substantial change in [proposals](proposals/README.md).
- Translate, draw, compose, improve the documents, or try a practice and report what you learned. Coding is optional.

The [Open Table Protocol](proposals/0001-open-table-protocol.md) explores how communities might cooperate across religious differences. It is a **draft**, not an adopted agreement or a description of any other religion's beliefs.

Participation follows our [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). Decisions follow [governance](GOVERNANCE.md). The [license](LICENSE.md) explains reuse and independent editions. Participation is public unless a channel explicitly says otherwise; do not post private disclosures or another person's personal information.
