# The Honest Revision

**Suggested rhythm: annually, or when a change calls for reflection.**

Name:

- A belief you changed.
- Something you misunderstood.
- One repair you still owe.

Ask: **"What do I understand differently, and what might I do about it?"**

Choose a small, concrete next step if one is appropriate. An apology, an improved habit, a corrected public claim, or a changed plan may matter more than a declaration of certainty.

The reflection can remain private. Sharing is optional, and confession must never become a means of control. No leader is entitled to a person's intimate history.
