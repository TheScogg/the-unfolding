# Making Room

**Suggested rhythm: when needed and within your capacity.**

Offer attention, resources, or space that helps another being flourish. This might mean listening carefully, restoring a habitat, preparing a meal, or stepping aside so someone else can lead.

Ask: **"Whose becoming could I make room for?"**

Let the recipient help define what is useful. An unwanted gift or an imposed explanation can crowd someone out instead of making room for them.

Give freely and respect your own limits. Your safety, dignity, health, and boundaries matter. Care includes accepting help and declining demands you cannot meet.
