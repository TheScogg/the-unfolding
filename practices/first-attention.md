# The First Attention

**Suggested rhythm: daily.**

Spend one quiet minute noticing something you usually overlook: a leaf, a familiar face, the water in your glass, or a sound outside your window.

Ask: **"What makes this possible?"**

You might notice the people, living beings, materials, and events on which this ordinary thing depends. You do not have to reach a religious conclusion.

No special feeling is required. Attention is enough. Shorten or change the practice when needed; there is no failure to confess if you miss a day.
