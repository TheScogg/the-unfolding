# Practices

Wonder, making, and care give the founding story a place in ordinary life. These practices are invitations, not obligations or measures of spiritual rank. Adapt them to your abilities, circumstances, and boundaries.

| Practice | A suggested rhythm | Invitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [The First Attention](first-attention.md) | Daily | Notice something until it becomes remarkable. |
| [The Small Making](small-making.md) | Weekly | Create or mend something. |
| [The Open Table](open-table.md) | Regularly | Share food, a wonder, a difficulty, or a question. |
| [Making Room](making-room.md) | When needed | Help another life flourish. |
| [The Honest Revision](honest-revision.md) | Annually | Let yourself change and name a repair. |

The [shared candle ceremony](one-light-many-lights.md) holds different interpretations around the same table.

## A daily rhythm

**Notice.** Pause for one minute and attend to something ordinary.

**Make.** Create or mend one small thing, letting it serve a life beyond your own.

**Give.** Offer time, kindness, or attention freely. The First Gift is an invitation, never a debt.

You do not need a group, a special vocabulary, an extraordinary experience, or a GitHub account to begin.
