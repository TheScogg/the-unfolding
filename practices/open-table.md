# The Open Table

**Suggested rhythm: regularly, as participants choose.**

Gather around a table. Share food, an honest question, and something that filled you with wonder. Make room for someone new.

## A possible gathering

1. Welcome everyone and explain that participation and silence are equally welcome.
2. Ask about food needs and offer what is available without making a contribution compulsory.
3. Invite each person to bring a wonder, a difficulty, or an unanswered question.
4. Listen without requiring agreement, advice, prayer, or personal disclosure.
5. If useful, choose one small act of care to do together.

People may observe, pass, decline a ritual, or leave. Ask before sharing a person's story outside the gathering. A person does not need to choose an interpretation, hold a particular belief, or use the language of this tradition to be treated with hospitality.

The [Open Table Protocol proposal](../proposals/0001-open-table-protocol.md) explores cooperation with other communities; it has not been adopted as an agreement.
