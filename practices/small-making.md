# The Small Making

**Suggested rhythm: weekly.**

Create or mend something: a meal, a drawing, a repaired chair, a piece of code, or a small improvement to a shared place.

Ask: **"What can I help become possible?"**

Let it serve a life beyond your own when you can. Occasionally share the story of making it, including what went wrong or surprised you. Finished, useful, beautiful, and experimental work all have a place.

There is no requirement to be productive to deserve dignity. Rest and receiving care belong in a life, too.
