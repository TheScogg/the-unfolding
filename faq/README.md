# Questions for new members

These answers introduce Church of the First Gift in its founding stage. The Unfolding is our origin story. Governance remains provisional; no established local congregation or membership registry is being announced here.

## Questions of belief

### Do I have to believe God still exists?

No. The Last Light, the Living Whole, and the Questioning share equal standing. Agreement about the giver is not a condition of belonging.

### Is the founding story scientifically proven?

No. The story gives spiritual meaning to cosmic origins. It does not demonstrate divine intention. Scientific questions remain open to scientific investigation and revision.

### Does prayer require someone to hear it?

The branches disagree. Prayer may be communion with a living divine presence, or remembrance, reflection, lament, and a promise to act. You may also choose silence.

### Why is there suffering in a sacred world?

This faith offers no settled explanation that makes suffering deserved or necessary. Care asks us to relieve suffering where we can; pain is never proof that someone lacks faith.

### What happens after death?

The shared tradition makes no promise of an afterlife. Members may hold different hopes. Grief, remembrance, and care for the living belong at the table.

### Must I take every detail literally?

No. Read the story as theology, sacred poetry, or a question worth living with. Discuss interpretations honestly and distinguish personal conviction from shared commitments.

## Questions of belonging

### How do I begin?

Try a minute of attention, make or mend something, and offer a little care. Read the story, ask questions, and gather with others if you wish. No initiation is required.

### Can I belong alongside another religion?

Within Church of the First Gift, yes. You need not erase your history or abandon another identity. Any obligations of another community are yours to consider.

### Does membership cost money?

No fee or required donation is part of this founding proposal. Any future shared expenses should be transparent and voluntary. Giving never buys status, spiritual authority, or access.

### Who gets to lead?

Leadership is service, not divine entitlement. Any future community should choose accountable facilitators, including the Questioning, and provide clear ways to challenge decisions and replace leaders.

### Do I have to sacrifice myself?

No. The First Gift invites care, not self-destruction. Your safety, dignity, health, and boundaries matter. Nobody may demand suffering, obedience, or intimacy as a test of faith.

### Can I disagree, change my mind, or leave?

Yes. You may decline a ritual, question a teaching, change branches, or leave without punishment or shunning. Friendship and respect should not depend on continued membership.
