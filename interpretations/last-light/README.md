# The Last Light

**God died so that we could live.**

In this reading, the Great Sacrifice was complete: the divine giver no longer exists. The universe is what remains possible because of that gift.

The sacred lies in the consequences of a generosity whose source is gone. Prayer can be remembrance, lament, gratitude, or a promise made among the living. Responsibility has passed into our hands.

This reading does not claim that death or suffering should be sought or demanded from anyone. The [covenant](../../covenant/README.md) protects dignity and freedom of conscience.

## Questions to live with

- Can a gift retain meaning when its giver is gone?
- What does gratitude mean when it cannot be returned to its source?
- How do we face uncertainty without promising rescue?

The [shared candle ceremony](../../practices/one-light-many-lights.md) gives this interpretation a voice beside other answers.
