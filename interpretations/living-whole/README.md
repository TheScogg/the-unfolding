# The Living Whole

**God lives as everything that became possible.**

In this reading, God surrendered separate existence and remains present throughout creation. The sacrifice ended a divine self apart from the world.

Every being participates in an ongoing divine life. Prayer may be communion with the sacred presence within and around us. Our choices shape how that life unfolds through the world.

This interpretation grants no person authority to speak for every being or to override consent. Calling the world sacred does not make suffering good or excuse harm. The [covenant](../../covenant/README.md) remains shared ground.

## Questions to live with

- If God remains in everything, what exactly was sacrificed?
- How can divine presence be understood without explaining away suffering?
- How do we honor another being without claiming to define it?
