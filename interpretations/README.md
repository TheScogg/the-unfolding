# The Great Question

**Did the giver survive the gift?**

The founding story leaves two serious possibilities and room to remain uncertain.

| | [The Last Light](last-light/README.md) | [The Living Whole](living-whole/README.md) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Central belief | God's sacrifice was complete. God no longer exists. | God surrendered separate existence and remains present throughout creation. |
| The sacred | We live among the consequences of a love whose source is gone. | Every being participates in an ongoing divine life. |
| Prayer | Remembrance, lament, gratitude, and promises among the living. | Communion with the divine presence within and around us. |
| Responsibility | The future rests with us; the gift has passed into our hands. | Our choices shape how divine life unfolds through the world. |
| Hard question | Can a gift retain meaning when its giver is gone? | If God remains in everything, what exactly was sacrificed? |

[The Questioning](questioning/README.md) keeps the question open. People who remain uncertain have equal standing and may help lead gatherings.

These positions are not a claim about existing denominations or an exhaustive list of permissible beliefs. People may change their minds, combine ideas, or propose other readings. None of these positions permits someone to override the [shared covenant](../covenant/README.md).

A sense of humor belongs here. So does the freedom to disagree.
