# The Questioning

**You may keep the question open.**

The Questioning do not settle whether God ceased to exist or ceased to exist separately. Uncertainty is a legitimate position, not a waiting room for full membership.

People who cannot choose have equal standing and may lead gatherings. They may find meaning in the story, question its premise, practice care, and continue listening. They may move toward another interpretation or remain uncertain throughout their lives.

## Questions to live with

- What can we share before we agree?
- What would count as a reason to change our minds?
- Can an unanswered question guide a meaningful practice?

In the [shared candle ceremony](../../practices/one-light-many-lights.md), the Questioning may remain silent or name the question they are carrying.

The [covenant](../../covenant/README.md) protects the right to doubt, decline, disagree, and leave.
