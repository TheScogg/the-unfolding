# The shared covenant

The gift is our shared foundation. The giver is our shared question.

## Five commitments

1. **Existence is a gift.** No person has to earn their right to exist. A life has dignity before it becomes useful.
2. **Care makes gratitude visible.** Treat people and the living world as bearers of the same gift. Let compassion shape what you do.
3. **Making is participation.** Art, cooking, repair, discovery, and useful work help creation become something further.
4. **Belief remains answerable to honesty.** Follow evidence. Let a better explanation change your mind. Questions belong within the tradition.
5. **A gift creates no debt of self-destruction.** Nobody owes their safety, dignity, health, or life to a leader or community. Boundaries belong here.

## Freedom of conscience

> No answer to the Great Question grants authority over another person.<br>
> No doubt removes a person from the table.<br>
> Every teaching may be questioned.<br>
> Every person may leave.

These commitments describe how this project seeks to treat people. Agreement about God's continued existence is not required. A person may decline any ritual, keep personal reflections private, change interpretations, or leave without punishment or shunning.

The freedom to question does not permit harassment or violations of another person's consent. See the [code of conduct](../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). The covenant governs participation; the separate [license](../LICENSE.md) governs reuse of publications and artwork.
