# The Great Sacrifice & The Unfolding

A 28-second creation story, 1280 x 720, 24 frames per second, with an original stereo ambient score. No stock visuals, stock recordings, samples, or voiceover are used.

The film was authored through the live EffectCraft MCP server with native shapes, text, glow effects, and keyframes. The editable project is [The-Unfolding.ecproj](The-Unfolding.ecproj). Its score is in [(Footage)/The-Unfolding-score.wav](%28Footage%29/The-Unfolding-score.wav); keep that folder beside the project when downloading it. The [score script](create_score.py) synthesizes the music with Python and NumPy.

## Story

- A solitary light: "Before the world, a gift."
- Contraction and surrender: "God surrendered a separate self."
- Expanding horizons and stars: "One became everything."
- A spiral cosmos and young worlds: "Light became stars. Stars made worlds."
- A growing tree: "The gift became life - and learned to care."
- The open circle: "God became the universe. We are what happens next."

The final H.264/AAC MP4 is available in [release 0.1.0](https://github.com/TheScogg/the-unfolding/releases/tag/v0.1.0), keeping the large rendered video out of the repository's source history. All 672 exported frames and the stereo audio were decoded and checked before publication.
