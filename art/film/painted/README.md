# The Unfolding: Painted Film Edition

A 42-second telling of the First Gift, in 1920 × 1080 at 24 frames per second. Luminous painted scenes move from divine light through surrender, worlds, and a living seedling, ending with Church of the First Gift and an open question.

## Visual description

A luminous gold lattice glows in an indigo void. Its light opens outward around an empty center, becoming filaments and stars. A blue world appears with atmosphere, oceans, and branching rivers. A river becomes a root; a seedling rises with gold running through its veins. The film closes on the open-circle emblem, the church's name, and the invitation: Wonder. Make. Care.

## Production and credits

The founding idea and editorial direction come from TheScogg. Four paintings were generated with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool; the exact prompts are recorded in [image-prompts.json](image-prompts.json). Motion was composed through the live EffectCraft MCP server using layered paintings, masks, parallax, particles, displacement, native paths, keyframes, and typography.

Narration uses **Daniel, a stock macOS synthetic voice**. No real person was cloned. The original synthesized ambient score and narration were mixed in SoundCraft, with music lowered beneath speech. The MP4 includes selectable English subtitles; the website uses the matching [WebVTT captions](narration.vtt). [Narration timing](narration-timing.json) is included for future translations.

The source archive contains the native EffectCraft project, four paintings, final stereo mix, and opening instructions. Keep `(Footage)` beside the project. All media paths are relative. Set your own output path before rendering.

All 1,008 frames were decoded, the scene contact sheet was visually checked, and the stereo audio and subtitle stream were verified before publication. See [verification.json](verification.json).
