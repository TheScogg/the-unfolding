# Art and editable sources

The open gold circle represents a divine self giving outward into creation. Midnight blue, warm gold, cream, and sage connect the publications and film. These illustrations are original contributions developed with AI assistance, not historical religious symbols claimed from another tradition.

## Portable graphics

- [Open-circle emblem](emblem.svg)
- [Cover illustration](cover.svg)
- [Cosmic unfolding](cosmos.svg)
- [The shared candle ceremony](candles.svg)
- [Tree of becoming](tree.svg)

SVG graphics can be edited in many vector tools. Textual source material is available throughout the repository as Markdown.

## Native sources

- [14-page field guide](field-guide.designcraft): DesignCraft; all artwork is stored in the document.
- [Six-panel brochure](brochure.vectorcraft): VectorCraft.
- [Cosmic field](first-gift.pcraft): layered PhotoCraft document.
- [Cosmic field PNG](first-gift.png): flattened illustration.
- [Film source and score](film/README.md): EffectCraft, with its audio in a relative subfolder.

Font files are not included. Publications were designed with Baskerville and Avenir Next; use your own licensed copies or substitute suitable fonts and check the layout. Native formats are provided for editability in addition to portable SVG, PDF, Markdown, and WAV files.
