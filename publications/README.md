# Publications

## Founding Edition 0.1.0

- [The Unfolding: A Field Guide to the First Gift](the-unfolding-field-guide.pdf): 14 illustrated pages, 7 x 9 inches, searchable text, in reading order. Covers the founding story, covenant, five practices, interpretations, candle ceremony, FAQ, and blessing. All pages were rendered and visually checked.
- [The Unfolding brochure](the-unfolding-brochure.pdf): six panels across two US Letter landscape pages. Print at actual size, duplex with short-edge flipping, and test a sheet before a print run. The panels are equal widths; a commercial roll-fold run should have its tuck panel adjusted by the printer. There is no bleed.
- [The Great Sacrifice & The Unfolding](https://github.com/TheScogg/the-unfolding/releases/tag/v0.1.0): the 28-second film and original ambient score, distributed as release assets.

Editable sources are in [art](../art/README.md). Readable source texts are in the founding-story, covenant, practices, interpretations, and FAQ directories. These documents introduce a proposed tradition; they do not document an already established institution or partner community.
